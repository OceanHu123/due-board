Supported institutions

Defaults are built in for the schools below — pick your school in Settings and the correct Canvas/Ed API URLs are already filled. Schools not listed use the Custom preset.

  • 🇦🇺 University of Sydney (USYD)
  • 🇦🇺 UNSW Sydney
  • 🇦🇺 University of Melbourne
  • 🇦🇺 Monash University
  • 🇦🇺 Australian National University (ANU)
  • 🇦🇺 University of Queensland (UQ)
  • ✨ Custom (any Canvas URL you configure)

How it works

  1. Sign in with a magic link (no password).
  2. Pick your institution — default Canvas/Ed URLs are seeded for you.
  3. Paste your own Canvas and Ed API tokens — stored encrypted on the server.
  4. Open the board anytime, or get email reminders at 08:00 / 18:00 (configurable).

Security

  • Tokens never ship to the browser after save; only ciphertext is stored.
  • Each account only sees its own dues.
  • Revoke tokens anytime in Canvas / Ed settings, and clear them here.
